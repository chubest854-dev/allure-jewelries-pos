# Allure Jewelries POS & Retail Management System 💍✨

A full-stack point-of-sale and store management application tailored for high-end boutique jewelry retail.

## Key Features
- **Cashier POS Interface:** Real-time catalog grid with instant search and category filter navigation.
- **Dynamic Gold Weight Scale:** Interactive gram scale calculator for weighted gold pieces based on live market rates per gram.
- **Automated Business Rules:** Automatic 5% VIP discount tier triggered on orders reaching or exceeding ₦1,000,000.
- **Cart & Order Controls:** Real-time stock reservation, quantity increments, and item removal.
- **Receipt Generation:** Thermal-style printable invoice modal with order timestamps and payment method tags.
- **Store Analytics & Restocking:** Daily, weekly, and monthly sales ledgers with low-stock warnings and one-click restock inputs.

## Tech Stack
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js
- **Frontend:** Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+), HTML5, CSS3
- **Data Store:** JSON-based persistent store
